## CN-0002 — Facebook comment: Ron Yeffet, Kushner, Jewish museum, Vlorë
- Submitted 23 October 2026, screenshot of Facebook comment.
- Claims: Ron Yeffet obtained defense tenders without competition; Kushner-linked Zvërnec project; Vlorë Jewish Museum; claims about a historical Jewish majority and an alleged future takeover of southern Albania.
- **Finding on Yeffet:** Exit investigation describes Timak Defence and Advanced Arms Technology, mixed private/state ownership and reported public payments. Media report SPAK searches and an investigation into suspected procurement irregularities. **The claims require contract-by-contract verification; no conviction established.**
- **Finding on museum:** AADF lists a $6.5m committed budget; published construction tender ceiling $3m excluding VAT; government identifies Kimmel Eshkolot Architects (Tel Aviv) as design winner. Cultural project, not evidence of a territorial transfer.
- **Unsubstantiated:** Alleged Jewish plan to take Vlorë or southern Albania; claim of 90% Jewish population without credible demographic evidence.

## CN-0003 — Facebook comment: emigration, housing, construction, Bektashi and governance
- Submitted 23 October 2026, screenshot of public Facebook comment.
- Claims: over one million emigrants; land concessions favor oligarchs; laundering through construction and unaffordable housing; Bektashi proposal creates a precedent for other states; clientelism, corruption, youth emigration and alleged pressure on young people.
- **Population:** INSTAT population decline is not equivalent to cumulative emigrant departures; Eurostat first residence permits cannot be added to diaspora ancestry counts. Verify precise period and metric.
- **Housing/money laundering:** FES study analyzes Albanian real estate, construction and laundering risks for 2015–2024; GI-TOC 2025 identifies regional real-estate vulnerabilities. These are systemic findings, not proof that every development is illicit.
- **Bektashi:** A sovereign entity was proposed; a proposed religious enclave does not establish a legal precedent for other sovereign states.
- **Unverified:** Claims of an intentional government policy to force youth to emigrate or a coordinated foreign territorial plan.
- Sources:
  - https://library.fes.de/pdf-files/bueros/albanien/22432.pdf
  - https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/money-laundering-in-the-western-balkans/
  - https://ocindex.net/assets/downloads/2025/english/ocindex_profile_albania_2025.pdf

## CN-0004 — Facebook comment: Bektashi, Vlorë projects, Vučić and Kosovo
- Received: 23 October 2026, public Facebook comment screenshot.
- Claim categories: proposed Bektashi sovereign entity; allegation that Vlorë projects (Zvërnec, Jewish Museum, port, airport) are being handed to Israelis; claims about Rama–Vučić cooperation, Russia sanctions and a genocide-related UN vote.
- Status: **Mixed: documented policy events and unsupported territorial/ownership conclusions**.
- **Bektashi:** Rama's proposal is documented, but a proposed entity is not proof of territorial transfer to other groups.
- **Vlorë projects:** The airport concession's company records list Mabco Constructions SA and 2A Group among participants, with Swiss/Kosovar corporate ties; they do not show a transfer to Israel. [Company profile](https://www.opencorporates.al/en/nuis/m11809002i), [concession record](https://www.opencorporates.al/sq/nuis/M11809002I).
- **Ron Yeffet airport allegation:** A report describes TIMAK AVIATION with Yeffet and Arjeta Puca, but explicitly says no official takeover decision or contract had been found. [Voxnews report](https://voxnews.al/english/aktualitet/prapaskenat-a-eshte-izraelit-ron-yeffet-njeriu-qe-edi-rama-po-pergati-i122149). **Do not draw an airport-award edge.**
- **Sazan:** Independent CORRECTIV review found no evidence of land sale to the Israeli state. [Fact-check](https://correctiv.org/faktencheck/hintergrund/2026/07/15/luxushotels-keine-hinweise-dass-albanien-land-an-israel-verkauft/).
- **Rama–Vučić:** Their Open Balkan cooperation is documented; assess policy outcomes and disagreements with Kosovo separately. [Regional research](https://www.bmlv.gv.at/pdf_pool/publikationen/sgi_rssee_prishtina_web_2025_06.pdf).
- **Srebrenica UN resolution:** The UN adopted a 2024 resolution designating 11 July as the international day of remembrance. **Correction, 23 October 2026:** Albania voted **YES** on 23 May 2024; Serbia voted NO and Israel did not vote. The claim that Albania did not vote is contradicted by the [UN recorded roll-call, A/RES/78/282](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4048673?ln=en).
- **Russian sanctions:** The comment's wording is ambiguous (Rama's support for Vučić not imposing sanctions versus Albania's own sanctions). Check dated official statements and EU alignment records rather than presenting the comment as a fact.
- **Unsubstantiated:** Claim that Israeli people or the Israeli state will take Vlorë or southern Albania.
- Editorial caution: Do not treat ethnicity or religion of a business owner as evidence of collective intent.
