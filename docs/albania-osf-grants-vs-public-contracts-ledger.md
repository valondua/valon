## 5. Interpretation and next public-record verification

OSFA acknowledges funding judicial reform, and 990-derived grant records show aggregate grants to OSFA. Public-contract and corruption records document separate payments or awards to construction/concession firms. **There is presently no documentary basis to connect those two financial streams as a single payment network.**

To improve the ledger without contacting anyone:
1. Retrieve original Form 990 Schedule I/F grants for 2020–2024, extracting individual grants and annual figures.
2. Retrieve OSFA annual financial statements and OSF grant database exports; note grants omitted from global databases because of local systems.
3. Cross-check US foreign-assistance awards for EWMI by recipient legal ID, award number, obligations and disbursements; do not confuse $6m and $9m figures reported in different articles.
4. Extract official Albanian procurement contract IDs, payments and vendor NUIS identifiers from APP, Treasury and Open Procurement Albania.
5. Join only by verified legal identifiers and financial document references; never join on a personal acquaintance, event attendance or surname.
6. Keep funding from the Swiss development agency for LëvizAlbania separate from Open Society's own funding.

**Publication:** All amounts retain currency, period, source type, and whether they are earmarks, grants, contracts, revenues or payments. Accusations are clearly labeled.
