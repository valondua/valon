# Albania–Israel: corporate, investment and institutional evidence register
**Research snapshot: 23 October 2026.** Public information only. This is a **non-exhaustive** corporate and institutional register, not a list of people categorized by Jewish identity or religion. Israeli citizenship, incorporation, contracts and government relationships are different attributes. Never infer unlawful influence from nationality, religion, association or a photograph.

## Aggregate figures — do not mix measures
- Albania's embassy in Israel reported **more than 45 Israeli companies** operating in the Albanian market, with investments in energy, agriculture, construction, pharmaceuticals, finance, cyber and security. Source: https://ambasadat.gov.al/israel/en/marredheniet-dypaleshe/
- A September 2026 Pamfleti investigation **claimed 105 Israeli companies**, but its count and definition have not been independently reconciled against QKB; treat as a press claim, not a certified number. https://www.pamfleti.net/english/dosja/rrjeti-izraelit-i-bizneseve-te-erreta-nen-mbrojtjen-e-edi-rames-i353402
- RTSH reports approximately **€19m Israeli investment in the first nine months of 2025**, and 2025 bilateral trade around **€37m** (€10.4m Albanian exports, €27m imports). Flow of investment and stock of FDI are not interchangeable. https://rtsh.al/rti/en/trade-between-albania-and-israel-rises-in-2025/
- **40 Israeli companies** participated in a May 12, 2026 Tirana summit, according to the Albanian government. Attendance does not establish registration, contracts or investments. https://www.kryeministria.al/en/newsroom/samiti-shqiperi-izrael-per-forcimin-e-bashkepunimit-ne-inovacion-teknologji-dhe-siguri-kibernetike/

## Real estate and media: what is and isn't established
- Corporate business scope covering real estate or construction **does not prove ownership of a specific parcel**. To attribute land, require ASHK title/parcel number, effective date, registered legal entity and transaction or lease.
- Israeli investor interest in Albanian property is reported, but a complete verified inventory of land/property holdings has **not** been assembled.
- Media Ownership Monitor and Albania's audiovisual regulator provide corporate and ultimate-owner registers. **No specific major Albanian broadcaster has been verified as Israeli-owned in this research round.** Do not infer ownership from editorial positions or the owner's surname.
- Sources: https://albania.mom-gmr.org/en/findings/findings/ ; https://ama.gov.al/krasniqi-at-the-62nd-epra-meeting-weve-completed-the-media-ownership-structure-transparency-and-the-study-on-audiovisual-media-service-providers-investments-beyond-the-media-market/?lang=en

## Bilateral institutional links
