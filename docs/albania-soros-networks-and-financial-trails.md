# Albania: Open Society Networks, Judicial Reform and Documented Financial Trails
Research snapshot: 23 October 2026. Public-source investigation only. Allegations are not findings of guilt.

## Open Society organizations and leadership
- **Open Society Foundations (OSF):** founded by George Soros; board chaired by Alex Soros. Board profile: https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/who-we-are/board-of-directors/alex-soros
- **Open Society Foundation for Albania (OSFA):** Albanian nonprofit NUIS J62005011N, registered 1996; Andi Dobrushi listed as legal representative. Registry: https://opencorporates.al/en/nipt/j62005011n
- **Open Society Foundations Western Balkans:** director Andi Dobrushi; deputy Miodrag Milosavljevic; COO Dukagjin Hyseni. Official roster: https://osfwb.org/who-we-are/
- **LëvizAlbania:** Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation-funded consortium implemented by OSFA, Partners Albania and Co-PLAN. This is a partnership, **not proof those two partner NGOs are owned by Soros**. https://www.levizalbania.al/en/about-us

## Documented work and public controversy
- OSF says its Albania operations began in 1992, supporting education, health, independent media, civil society, EU integration and judicial reform. It reports earmarking $600,000 in 2015 for legal experts, research, public outreach and conferences on justice reform. https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/newsroom/open-society-foundations-albania
- Exit's 2016 examination of leaked OSFA 2014–17 strategy documents reported plans to shape constitutional/judicial reform and discussions with Rama's incoming government. This supports a political advocacy/engagement edge, **not personal control of judges**. https://exit.al/en/constitutional-reform-conceived-planned-and-carried-out-by-soros/
- Judicial Watch's 2018 FOIA-derived report alleges US taxpayer-funded support for Soros-linked work influencing judicial reform and criticizes the political consequences. Source and disclosed records: https://www.judicialwatch.org/judicial-watch-doj-records-show-obama-administration-helped-fund-george-soros-left-wing-political-activities-albania/ ; https://www.judicialwatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Judicial-Watch-Special-Report-Open-Society-Foundations-2018-1.pdf
- OSF's 2025 response denies that OSF receives USAID funding or directs USAID's budgets, while acknowledging cooperation with government and other donors. This may concern **different legal entities and funding flows** than the grants Judicial Watch describes; reconcile award numbers, recipients and dates before treating claims as mutually exclusive. https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/newsroom/false-claims-regarding-open-society-george-soros-and-usaid
- **No verified direct Soros-to-Rama personal payment, private company ownership, or award of Albanian public tenders to George/Alex Soros has been established by the reviewed documents.** This is a statement of research limits, not proof none exist.

## Money-trail case 1: 5D Construction / Tirana
- Transparency International's case summary says former municipal officials Redi Molla and Mariglen Qato allegedly controlled 5D Construction through nominal owners while institutions under their influence awarded it public contracts. It cites about €20m in company revenues for 2016–2022, much of it from public contracts. Proceedings and seizures are documented, but a final conviction is not established here. https://www.transparency.org/en/cepi/cases/5d-case-senior-public-officials-accused-of-embezzling-public-contract-funds
- Open Data Albania's dossier lists seized entities/assets including 5D Konstruksion, Restur and a 24% interest in Mediterranean Investment Group held through Restur. https://ndiqparate.al/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/5D-English.pdf
- **Trace:** municipal procurement authority → 5D Construction (contract award) → alleged indirect owners Molla/Qato → seized corporate/property interests. Each arrow is tagged as contract, allegation or court seizure; **not a link to Rama personally**.

## Money-trail case 2: Waste incinerators
- Transparency International documents three concessions, SPAK's cases involving Lefter Koka, Arben Ahmetaj and intermediaries, and allegations of at least €491,000 in benefits to Ahmetaj. The allegations against Ahmetaj remain distinct from a conviction; he denies wrongdoing. https://www.transparency.org/en/cepi/cases/albania-arben-ahmetaj-deputy-pm-involved-inincinerators-concession-scandal-
- **Trace:** public contracting ministries → concessionaires and related firms → alleged intermediary transactions → public officials named by SPAK. The exact contractual and payment edges require source documents; **no direct criminal-payment edge to Rama established**.

## Analytical separation
The 5D and incinerator cases **do not establish financial connections to OSFA or Alex/George Soros**. Place them in parallel, distinct network layers unless a primary source identifies a transaction. Do not infer shared criminality from meetings, awards, political affiliation, religion or philanthropy.
