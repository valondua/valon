# Annual population correction — 24 October 2026

ACQJ's 4 June 2026 article is a useful research lead but its headline substitutes net migration loss for total population decline. R55 corrects this using the linked INSTAT release (2 June 2026) and the prior-year census-rebased release (12 June 2025).

Sources:
- https://www.acqj.al/en/shqiperia-vazhdon-te-tkurret-humbi-mbi-28-mije-banore-ne-2025/
- https://www.instat.gov.al/media/cc0kwohw/popullsia-1-janar-2026.pdf
- https://www.instat.gov.al/media/y5diizmw/finale-popullsia-1-janar-2025-dt-12062025.pdf

Reconciliation:
- Residents 1 January 2025: 2,363,314; 1 January 2026: 2,335,930.
- Difference: −27,384, or −1.1587% (reported −1.16%).
- 2025 births 22,541 minus deaths 21,394 = natural increase 1,147.
- Natural increase +1,147 plus net migration −28,531 = −27,384.
- The article's “nearly 25” ratio is net migration loss / natural increase, not emigrants / births. It is not used as a departure statistic.

INSTAT's annual methodology uses cohort components and estimates net migration mainly from the Labour Force Survey migration module with population-projection coefficients. It is not a gross border-departure count. The new annual estimate remains separate from the 2011/2023 census chart, EU cumulative permit issuances and US diaspora estimates.

Additional checked context: median age 45 vs 44.3; Tirana region +0.7%, 32.8% of residents; Kukës −2.59% and Shkodër −2.56%. These are regional changes, not the number emigrating from each region. ACQJ's separate KLSH policy-audit claims are not independently verified in this release, and are explicitly identified as such rather than imported as proven findings.
