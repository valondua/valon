# Valon Asani editorial voice

## The through-line

**Be the one. Know your roots. Build what comes next.** Speak as a founder and a person living between Kosovo and Switzerland, not as a spokesman for every Albanian. Start with a real moment or question, say what you believe and why, and leave the reader with a concrete choice they can make. Be direct, warm, curious and candid. Avoid motivational slogans without a lived example.

Three recurring paths give the website and social stories a recognisable shape:

1. **Love well.** Dating, boundaries, mutual respect, family and the conditions for lasting relationships. Challenge manipulation, betrayal and control disguised as care or family honour. dua.com is a way for Albanians across borders to meet; partnership and parenthood are personal choices, not obligations.
2. **Live your values.** Besa, keeping your word, discipline, health and accountability. Challenge empty status, broken promises, shame and advice that asks people to abandon self-respect. Describe personal practices as experience, not a universal treatment or scientific finding.
3. **Carry our roots forward.** Albanian language, stories, culture, Kosovo, diaspora and the ability to build institutions and opportunities. Challenge documented repression, denial of rights, corruption, cultural erasure and specific false claims. Name a policy, action or responsible actor and the evidence. Never cast Serbs, members of a religion or any other entire group as the enemy. Our identity is carried by people, language, memory and choices; it is not a claim of genetic purity or inherited superiority.

Business and technology stories belong to one of these paths when they show what Valon is building and for whom. Travel and family notes may simply be personal moments; do not force a national message into every post.

## Each article

- Keep the source post, original language, publication date and attribution. Embed or link the original social post. A caption is a starting point, not a transcript of the video.
- Give the article its own clear question or claim. Use an opening scene, a developed idea, and a practical ending. Short source notes should remain labelled as notes until a recording, transcript or Valon's notes support an essay.
- Distinguish **I experienced**, **I believe**, and **the evidence shows**. Verify health, money, law, history and current-event claims with primary sources. Give context to statistics and avoid invented quotations.
- Preserve the substance and voice across English, Albanian and Swiss Standard German. Keep Albanian idiom natural; write German in Swiss standard style (for example, `ss` rather than `ß`). Publish language alternatives only when real translations exist and have been reviewed.
- Use a relevant, non-repeated image with rights and alt text. Link to a related story in the same path and offer the appropriate newsletter or conversation next step.
- Save substantive edits and translations as drafts, review the exact copy and sources, then publish. Keep legacy URLs and dates unless the content genuinely changed enough to justify a visible update note.
