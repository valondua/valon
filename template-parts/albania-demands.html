Initial organiser platform

Issued by / participants
Organisers at the Tirana rally, as reported by Boldnews
Event / platform date
4 June 2026
Reporting date
4 June 2026 — Boldnews

Attributed paraphrase

Organisers called for the government’s resignation; repeal of strategic-investor status and its legal framework and of the Mountain Package; and reversal of amendments to protected-area and cultural-heritage laws.

This is the platform attributed to the rally organisers, not evidence that every participant endorsed it. Reversing amendments is distinct from repealing the protection laws themselves.

Expanded coordinating-group platform

Issued by / participants
Protest Coordinating Group
Event / platform date
22 June 2026
Reporting date
22 June 2026 — Lapsi; letter publicised 26 June 2026 — Ora News

Attributed paraphrase

The group called for the government’s resignation; a nonpartisan technical transitional government with a 12-month mandate; constitutional and electoral reform, including no more than two full or partial prime-ministerial terms over an individual’s political career; reversal of the disputed legislation; and a new citizen–state social contract.

The disputed legislation refers to strategic-investor status/framework, the Mountain Package and amendments to protected-area and cultural-heritage laws. This is the coordinating group’s platform, not a position established for every protester.
