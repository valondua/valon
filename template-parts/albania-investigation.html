Verified record

Albania → NATO

Member since 1 April 2009

Albania joined NATO on 1 April 2009. Its collective-security commitments differ from Switzerland’s permanent neutrality. The research asks whether foreign-policy choices offer measurable humanitarian, diplomatic, economic and security benefits proportionate to their risks.

Neutrality is not the absence of sanctions, diplomacy or refugee protection. The comparison is a political alternative to analyse, not evidence that Albania is legally required to follow Swiss policy.

Identify each parcel and beneficial owner; trace title transfers and valuations; obtain contracts, duration of land rights, state guarantees, tax treatment, environmental assessments, permit decisions and judicial records. No such plan is established by the evidence presented here. A historical speech, a Bektashi proposal and resort investments do not by themselves demonstrate a common territorial project. Religious or ethnic identity is not evidence of coordination, ownership, wrongdoing or protest financing.
