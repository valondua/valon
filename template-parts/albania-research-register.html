Research update · 24 October 2026

Follow the records. Test the connections.

Land, ownership, public money, diplomacy and the claims behind the debate.

This update brings together the new research on public decisions, companies, money and community claims. Each entry separates what its sources support from what remains unresolved. Older case reports are dated evidence, not a claim that the latest court outcome has been verified.

Corrections made before publication: Albania’s UN vote; the World Bank brief’s version and poverty estimates; the AIDA register’s purpose; the OSF grant claims; and the distinction between 5D contracts and payments. Read the correction entries below.

“Documented record” means the described document supports that limited claim. “Official account” attributes an institution’s statement. “Reported relationship” and “Reported findings” rely on reporting or summaries. Allegations, disputed claims and open questions are not findings of guilt.
